Stripe does not support businesses based in Jamaica. Several merchants of record do pay out to Jamaica, so you can still sell software, SaaS and digital products to buyers abroad. Facts below come from each provider's own pages, checked on 2026-11-19.

## Is Stripe available in Jamaica?

No. Jamaica is not on Stripe's [global availability page](https://stripe.com/global), which listed 51 countries when we checked. Jamaica is also not on the list of supported business locations for [Stripe Managed Payments](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/managed-payments/eligibility), Stripe's own merchant of record product.

## Merchants of record for Jamaican sellers

A merchant of record sells to your customer, collects and remits sales tax and VAT, handles chargebacks and pays you out. You do not need a Stripe account of your own.

| Provider | Jamaica status (2026-11-19) | What the provider says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Polar](/provider/polar/) | Listed | Jamaica is on Polar's [payout country list](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/supported-countries). Payouts go through Stripe Connect Express, and Polar says Stripe Payments does not need to be available in your country. |
| [Dodo Payments](/provider/dodo-payments/) | Listed | Jamaica is on Dodo's [accepted countries list](https://docs.dodopayments.com/miscellaneous/accepted-countries-and-territories). Eligibility follows the country that issued your government ID, not where your company is registered. |
| [Paddle](/provider/paddle/) | Not excluded | Jamaica is not on Paddle's [unsupported countries list](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle). Paddle says it works with software businesses anywhere else. |
| [Lemon Squeezy](/provider/lemon-squeezy/) | Closed to new sellers | Jamaica is on Lemon Squeezy's [bank payout list](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries), but Lemon Squeezy [closed new store sign-ups](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/blog/saying-goodbye-to-lemon-squeezy) on November 16, 2026 and stops processing payments on March 31, 2027. |
| Creem | Not listed | Jamaica is not on Creem's [supported country list](https://docs.creem.io/merchant-of-record/supported-countries). Creem says you cannot use it yet if your country is not listed. |

If you already sell through Lemon Squeezy from Jamaica, plan a move before March 31, 2027. Stripe Managed Payments, the replacement Lemon Squeezy points to, does not list Jamaica as a business location, so Polar, Dodo or Paddle are the options on this page.

## How you get paid

- **Polar:** Polar passes on Stripe's payout fees without markup: $2 per month with active payouts, 0.25% + 25 cents per payout, and currency conversion from 0.25% in the EU to 1% elsewhere. You start withdrawals yourself.
- **Dodo Payments:** standard payouts are free, with a $5 fee on payouts under $1,000. Dodo lists $25 for USD SWIFT payouts to non-US businesses.
- **Paddle:** pays monthly once your balance passes your threshold (minimum $100), by wire transfer or Payoneer, and sends payouts by the 15th. A wire in a currency that differs from your bank's country goes by SWIFT for $15. Payoneer payouts carry Payoneer's own fees.

We did not find a Jamaica-specific payout fee on any of these pages. Your bank may charge to receive an international wire, so ask it before you choose.

## The US LLC route

Some founders form a US company to open US banking and payment accounts.

- **Mercury:** Jamaica is not on Mercury's [prohibited countries page](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28771710754580-Prohibited-countries), which is based on where you live. Mercury needs a US company and reviews each application.
- **Airwallex:** Jamaica is not on Airwallex's [eligible countries list](https://help.airwallex.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4408591334937-Eligible-Countries-and-Territories) for business registration. A US company can apply, since the United States is listed.

Formation services we track: [doola](/provider/doola/), [Firstbase](/provider/firstbase/) and [Northwest Registered Agent](/provider/northwest-registered-agent/). We have not verified on their sites whether each accepts founders living in Jamaica. A US company brings US filing duties and may have Jamaican tax consequences, so speak to a qualified accountant first. See [US bank account for a non-resident LLC](/guides/us-bank-account-for-non-resident-llc/).

## Banking and payouts

- **Wise:** Jamaica is not on Wise's [list of places it does not serve](https://wise.com/help/articles/2978049/where-can-i-use-wise). Wise says it will tell you whether it supports businesses in your country when you [add your details](https://wise.com/help/articles/2977974/can-my-business-use-wise).
- **Payoneer:** unknown. We have not verified Payoneer's rules for Jamaica on its site.

## Which option fits

- Selling SaaS or software with a mix of US and non-US buyers: compare Polar and Dodo, which both list Jamaica.
- Want one flat rate with no international card surcharge on the pricing page: Paddle, which does not exclude Jamaica.
