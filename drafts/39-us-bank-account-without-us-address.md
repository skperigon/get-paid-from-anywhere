A US company has more than one address, and providers treat each one differently. Most founders abroad can get a US legal address from a registered agent. The address that usually decides the application is the operating address: where the business is actually run. Facts below come from each provider's own pages, checked on 2026-11-27. Rules change and every application is reviewed, so verify with the provider before you rely on anything here.

## The three addresses providers ask for

- **Legal or registered address:** the address on your formation documents. A registered agent's address is often used here.
- **Operating address:** where you and your team work day to day. Providers call it the physical address, principal place of business or trading address. This is the one virtual addresses fail.
- **Your personal address:** where each owner lives. It is checked for identity and against country lists.

Some providers also ask for a mailing address for cards.

## Mercury

Mercury's [address page](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28769699533588-Company-address-requirements) lists three:

- **Legal address:** from your formation documents and used as the card billing address. It must be a physical address in the United States, such as the address on your formation documents or an office. A PO box is not accepted.
- **Physical address:** where you work. Mercury accepts a verifiable residential address if you have no office yet, and verifiable international addresses for overseas customers. It does not accept a PO box, virtual address, commercial mail receiving agency, mail center or registered agent address. Mercury may ask you to verify this address if your legal address is a PO box, virtual address, mail center or registered agent.
- **Mailing address:** where Mercury sends physical cards. It must be a physical address the US Postal Service can deliver to.

To verify an address, Mercury lists documents such as a government ID, a signed lease, a utility bill or a non-Mercury bank statement from the last 90 days, a coworking invoice, or recent tax documents. The document must show the company or owner's name and the full address.

Mercury still needs existing or planned US operations, and it does not accept founders who live in countries on its prohibited list. See [Mercury for non-US residents](/guides/mercury-for-non-us-residents/).

## Airwallex

Airwallex's [US documents page](https://help.airwallex.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4419267227417-Documents-required-for-companies-in-the-US) is the strictest of the four on this point:

- The principal place of business must be a genuine, physical business location in the US, where day-to-day management and operations take place.
- A registered agent's address, including one from a company formation service, can be your registered (legal) address, but for most businesses not your principal place of business.
- Two exceptions allow a registered agent's address as the principal place of business: the business sells goods online, or it was incorporated within the last year. The exceptions do not cover PO boxes, virtual mailboxes or mailbox rental services.
- The principal place of business field only accepts a US address. You can enter your residential address outside the US in your personal details, but Airwallex says that does not replace the US requirement.

If you do not qualify for an exception and have no genuine US place of business, Airwallex says you may not be eligible, and you can apply again once you have one.

## Relay

Relay's [registered agent and operating address page](https://relayfi.com/hc/en-us/articles/23792837830676-Using-a-Registered-Agent-Address-and-Operating-Address-for-Your-Business/) (updated August 11, 2026) says:

- A registered agent address can be your legal address, including for non-US residents setting up a US business.
- You still need an operating address. A home address, domestic or international, or a physical office is acceptable. Registered agent addresses, PO boxes, virtual mailboxes and mailbox rental services are not.
- Every owner gives a verifiable personal residential address, even when the business uses a registered agent.
- One address cannot serve every role. A registered agent address cannot double as an operating or personal address.

Relay's [required documents page](https://relayfi.com/hc/en-us/articles/38049251551636-Required-Documents-and-Organization-Details-to-Open-a-Relay-Account-by-Entity-Type/) (updated November 12, 2026) lists a "physical U.S. address" for every beneficial owner, with no PO boxes or virtual mailboxes. That conflicts with the address page, so whether a non-US home address is enough at Relay is unknown. Ask Relay before you apply. Relay also blocks owners with citizenship or residency in countries on its prohibited list.

## Wise Business

Wise's [US requirements page](https://wise.com/us/blog/wise-business-account-requirements-us) (last updated 31 July 2026) asks for two business addresses:

- **Registered address:** where the business is incorporated. Wise says it can be the address of a registration agency or your attorney.
- **Trading address:** your primary place of business, such as an office or coworking space. It must be a physical location. PO boxes, registration agencies, mail forwarding services and similar virtual addresses are not accepted.

Each owner and the controller also gives a full residential address. The page does not say whether the trading address may be outside the US, so we mark that unknown. Separately, Wise's USD account details depend on your address country. The [US bank account guide](/guides/us-bank-account-for-non-resident-llc/) covers that list.

## Before you apply

- Use the registered agent's address only where the provider allows it, usually the legal address.
- Give your real operating address. For Mercury and Relay that can be your home or office abroad. For Airwallex it must be in the US unless an exception applies.
- Have a recent document that shows the name and the full address, such as a lease, a utility bill or a bank statement.
- Check where every 25% owner lives against each provider's country list. An accepted address does not override a country ban.
- Expect manual review and requests for more documents.
