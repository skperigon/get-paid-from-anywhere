A merchant of record (MoR) sells your product to the customer on your behalf. It processes the payment, handles sales tax and VAT where it covers them, and pays you after its fees. For an AI app the main question is not fees. It is whether the MoR will approve the product at all.

This page covers AI software, AI content generators, chatbots and AI features sold as SaaS. For general subscription software, see [Merchant of record for SaaS](/sell/saas/). For files and templates, see [Merchant of record for digital products](/sell/digital-products/). Facts were checked on 2026-11-11 on each provider's own pages. Policies change often, so verify with the provider before you apply.

## What each provider says about AI

### Paddle

Paddle's [acceptable use page](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/what-am-i-not-allowed-to-sell-on-paddle) (last updated 13 April 2026 on that page) says Paddle is built for software companies, including B2B SaaS, consumer software and games. It has no general ban on AI software.

Its prohibited list has a content generation section. It names:

- Human-like faces in realistic, stylized or animated forms (marked as a restricted category).
- Face swaps.
- Deepfake images or videos.
- Voice impersonations.
- Any content generation using a person's likeness without that person's explicit consent.
- Automated decision-making or categorization of people.
- Any content generation that falls into other prohibited categories.

Restricted categories get enhanced due diligence, and Paddle says its team will reach out for more information if needed. Other prohibited items that often touch AI products include automated social media marketing that claims to increase engagement, captcha solving, and medical advice such as weight loss or muscle building guidance.

### Lemon Squeezy

Lemon Squeezy's [prohibited products page](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/prohibited-products) lists software and SaaS as acceptable and does not mention AI as a category. The only AI-specific item is in the first prohibited line: sexually oriented or pornographic content of any kind, including NSFW chatbots.

Other prohibited items that can apply to AI products: services of any kind, products you do not hold a proper license or IP rights for, and anything restricted by its payment partners. The [activate-your-store docs](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/activate-your-store) say every store goes through KYC and KYB review, approvals typically take 2 to 3 business days, and SaaS and software companies that use subscriptions or license keys are typically allowed. If you are unsure, Lemon Squeezy asks you to contact support before you start selling.

### Polar

Polar's [acceptable use policy](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/acceptable-use) (effective date on that page: October 1, 2026) lists software and SaaS as acceptable. AI shows up in three places.

Prohibited:

- Adult content and services, including adult AI-generated content and AI relationship services.
- Content generation that infringes on trademarks or copyrights, enables face swaps or deep fakes, or produces adult content.

Restricted, needing closer review (approval depends on the product, business maturity, ownership and funding, safeguards and risk profile, and may not be granted):

- AI content generation tools (text, image, video, voice).
- Audio, music and chatbot generators, with no voice cloning, NSFW, IP infringement or fake claims.

Polar also prohibits reselling software licenses without authorization, financial trading bots and trading signals, and fake testimonials or review inflation platforms.

### Dodo Payments

Dodo Payments' [merchant acceptance policy](https://docs.dodopayments.com/miscellaneous/merchant-acceptance) is the only one of the five that names AI as a welcomed category: "SaaS & AI products" is first on its list of businesses it supports.

Its prohibited list names several AI cases:

- NSFW and adult content, whether real or AI-generated, including NSFW chatbots.
- Digital products with limited or unclear value, with AI-generated PDFs with little value as an example.
- AI-generated relationship companions, under social matching services.
- Reselling AI APIs, datasets or tools without clear rights or authorization.

Categories that often require review:

- AI content generation tools (text, image, video, voice), with no impersonation, scraping or deepfakes.
- Audio, music and chatbot generators, with no voice cloning, NSFW, IP infringement or fake claims.

Dodo says it may ask for extra details such as disclaimers, demo access or policy links before approval, and that approval is not guaranteed. It invites sellers near the edge of a category to contact compliance@dodopayments.com before applying.

### Stripe Managed Payments

Standard Stripe Payments leaves you as the merchant. Stripe's Managed Payments is a separate MoR product. Its [eligibility page](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/managed-payments/eligibility) supports software, video games, digital media, online courses and electronically supplied business and web services. It does not mention AI by name. Products must be fully automated, and professional services such as consulting, design, development or tech support are not supported.

Access needs an eligibility review, and your business must be based in a listed location: the US, Canada, a list of European countries, Australia, Hong Kong, Japan or Singapore. Founders elsewhere cannot use it.

## Fees on an AI subscription

Most AI apps bill monthly, so subscription surcharges matter. Here is what each fee page said on 2026-11-11.

| Fee | Paddle | Lemon Squeezy | Polar | Dodo Payments |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Base rate | 5% + 50 cents | 5% + 50 cents | Starter 5% + 50 cents; Pro 3.8% + 40 cents ($20/mo); Growth 3.6% + 35 cents ($100/mo); Scale 3.4% + 30 cents ($400/mo) | 4% + 40 cents (US domestic cards and wallets) |
| Subscription surcharge | None listed | +0.5% | None on Starter or paid plans. Early Member (orgs created before May 27, 2026): +0.5% | +0.5% for subscriptions, addons and usage-based billing |
| International cards | None listed | +1.5% outside the US | +1.5% for non-US cards | +1.5% for cards and APMs outside the US |
| PayPal | None listed | +1.5% | Not listed on the fees page | +3% |
| Monthly platform fee | None | None listed | None on Starter; paid plans as above | None |

Sources: [Paddle pricing](https://www.paddle.com/pricing), [Lemon Squeezy fees](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees), [Polar fees](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/fees), [Dodo pricing](https://dodopayments.com/pricing).

Dodo's pricing page also says usage-based billing for models like token usage and customer credits is included, with the 0.5% billing add-on above.

### Worked example: $20 per month AI subscription

Our own arithmetic on the published rates, on a $20 price with no tax added. Lemon Squeezy and Polar calculate fees on the total order value, so tax added at checkout raises the fee.

| $20 monthly charge | Paddle | Lemon Squeezy | Polar Starter | Polar Early Member | Dodo Payments |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| US card | $1.50 | $1.60 | $1.50 | $1.30 | $1.30 |
| Non-US card | $1.50 | $1.90 | $1.80 | $1.60 | $1.60 |

How those numbers were built:

- Paddle: 5% + 50 cents on both rows.
- Lemon Squeezy: 5% + 0.5% subscription + 50 cents. Non-US adds 1.5%.
- Polar Starter: 5% + 50 cents. Non-US adds 1.5%.
- Polar Early Member: 4% + 0.5% subscription + 40 cents. Non-US adds 1.5%.
- Dodo: 4% + 0.5% subscription + 40 cents. Non-US adds 1.5%.

Model API costs are not included. For many AI apps those costs are larger than the MoR fee, so price with both in mind.

## Before you apply

- Describe the product plainly on your site: what it generates, what inputs it takes, and what it will not do. Dodo and Polar both review AI generators case by case.
- Check the restricted lists for face, voice and likeness features. Paddle, Polar and Dodo all prohibit deepfakes or impersonation.
- Avoid adult features if you want broad MoR options. Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar and Dodo all prohibit adult content, and Polar and Dodo also name AI relationship companions.
- Make sure you have the rights to the model or data you build on. Unauthorized resale is prohibited on Paddle, Polar and Dodo.
- Check country support. Acceptance of the product does not mean your country is supported. See [Lemon Squeezy supported countries](/guides/lemon-squeezy-supported-countries/) and [Dodo Payments supported countries](/guides/dodo-payments-supported-countries/), or [browse by country](/countries/).

We do not give legal or tax advice. This page reports what each provider publishes. Verify with the provider before you rely on any rule or figure.
