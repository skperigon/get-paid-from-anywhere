Gumroad works in Pakistan: it pays out to Pakistani bank accounts in rupees. Pakistani sellers still look elsewhere for two reasons. The fees are higher than most alternatives, and Gumroad does not allow AI services. Fewer alternatives accept Pakistani sellers than Indian ones: Stripe and Dodo Payments do not. Below is what Gumroad and the other providers say on their own pages, checked on 2026-11-22. Policies change and approval is never guaranteed, so verify with the provider before you rely on any option.

This page is not tax, legal or banking advice. Links to providers on this site are not affiliate links today. See the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) for how that would be marked.

## What Gumroad offers Pakistani sellers

- **Payouts:** Pakistan is on Gumroad's [bank payout list](https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid), paid in PKR to a local bank account. Gumroad says it can only pay out in your local currency, so it will not pay a Pakistani seller in USD. It does not pay out to Payoneer or Wise. The minimum payout is $100. Bank payouts outside its named country groups go out on Tuesdays, and on weekly, monthly or quarterly schedules each sale is held for at least 7 days first. For prompt payouts, Gumroad says you need a government photo ID and proof of residence in Pakistan, or a business registered there.
- **Tax:** Gumroad says it has been the [merchant of record](https://gumroad.com/pricing) for all sales since January 1, 2025 and handles sales tax collection and remittance.
- **Fees:** 10% + 50 cents per direct sale. Gumroad's [fees page](https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees) says card processing (2.9% + 30 cents) and PayPal fees are not included. Sales through its Discover marketplace cost a flat 30%. Once your paid sales pass $20,000 in a calendar month, new direct sales that month drop to 5% + 50 cents.
- **Rules:** Gumroad's [list of things not allowed](https://gumroad.com/help/article/155-things-you-cant-sell-on-gumroad) includes AI services (access to AI tools, chatbots, image or content generation services), web hosting, physical products and event tickets.

## How you get paid

- **Gumroad:** bank payout in PKR, $100 minimum, held at least 7 days on weekly, monthly or quarterly schedules. Gumroad says bank payouts take 2 to 7 business days to process, depending on the country.
- **Polar:** Polar passes on Stripe's [payout fees](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/fees): $2 per month with active payouts, 0.25% + 25 cents per payout, and currency conversion of up to 1% outside the EU. You start withdrawals yourself.
- **Creem:** pays out on the 1st and 15th of each month, to a bank account or a USDC wallet. Its supported countries page lists a local bank payout fee of 7 EUR/USD or 1% of the payout, whichever is higher.
- **Paddle:** pays monthly once your balance passes your threshold (minimum $100), by wire transfer or Payoneer, and sends payouts by the 15th. Paddle's [payout fee page](https://www.paddle.com/help/manage/get-paid/is-there-a-fee-taken-for-payouts) says a wire in a currency that differs from your bank's country goes by SWIFT for $15. Its pages do not say whether it pays out in PKR, so whether that fee applies to a Pakistani bank is unknown. Payoneer payouts carry Payoneer's own fees. Payoneer publishes a [registration guide for individuals in Pakistan](https://www.payoneer.com/resources/country-guides/payoneer-individual-account-in-pakistan/).

Your bank may also charge to receive an international transfer. We did not verify State Bank of Pakistan or FBR rules for any of these options. Speak to a qualified accountant before you choose.

## Product rules to check

- **AI tools:** Gumroad does not allow AI services. Polar accepts software and SaaS but lists AI content generation tools among products that need closer review before approval, per its [acceptable use policy](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/acceptable-use). See [selling AI apps](/sell/ai-apps/) for more.
- **eBooks and templates:** Gumroad allows them if you created them, but not resale of products you did not create. Polar lists eBooks as needing closer review.
- **Services:** Gumroad does not allow services fulfilled outside Gumroad. Polar says it is not designed for businesses whose main offering is human services. We have not checked Creem's or Paddle's service rules for this page, so check before you apply.

## Which option fits

- Selling courses, eBooks or creator products to an audience that already finds you on Gumroad: Gumroad's PKR bank payouts may outweigh the higher fee.
- Selling SaaS or AI tools: compare Polar, Creem and Paddle, which all accept Pakistani sellers and allow software.
- Want one flat rate with no international card surcharge: Creem or Paddle.
- Want to be paid through Payoneer: Paddle offers it as a payout method. Gumroad does not.
