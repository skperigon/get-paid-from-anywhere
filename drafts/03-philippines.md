If you are a Filipino developer or founder selling software, templates or online courses to buyers abroad, Stripe is not an option at home. The good news is that most of the main merchants of record will pay out to the Philippines. Here is what each provider's own pages said on 2026-10-22.

## Is Stripe available in the Philippines?

No. The Philippines is not on Stripe's [global availability page](https://stripe.com/global). Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Hong Kong and Indonesia (marked Preview) are listed, but the Philippines is not.

## Merchants of record that pay out to the Philippines

A merchant of record sells to your customer on your behalf, handles sales tax and VAT, and then pays you. Because the merchant of record is the seller, Stripe does not need to be available in your country.

| Provider | Philippines status (2026-10-22) | What the provider says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Paddle](/provider/paddle/) | Not excluded | The Philippines is not on Paddle's [unsupported countries list](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle). Paddle says it works with software businesses. |
| [Lemon Squeezy](/provider/lemon-squeezy/) | Listed | The Philippines is on Lemon Squeezy's [bank payout list](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries). |
| [Polar](/provider/polar/) | Listed | The Philippines is on Polar's [payout country list](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/supported-countries). Polar pays out through Stripe Connect Express. |
| [Dodo Payments](/provider/dodo-payments/) | Listed | The Philippines is on Dodo's [accepted countries list](https://docs.dodopayments.com/miscellaneous/accepted-countries-and-territories). Dodo checks the country of your government ID. |

Headline fees, from each provider's pricing page: [Paddle](https://www.paddle.com/pricing) 5% + 50 cents per transaction; [Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing) 5% + 50 cents; [Polar](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/fees) 5% + 50 cents on its free plan, plus 1.5% for non-US cards; [Dodo](https://dodopayments.com/pricing) 4% + 40 cents for US cards, plus 1.5% for international cards. See [Polar vs Lemon Squeezy](/compare/polar-vs-lemon-squeezy/) for a closer look at two of them.

## Local payment gateways

If most of your customers are in the Philippines, a local gateway can take GCash and QR payments. Xendit's [pricing page](https://www.xendit.co/en/pricing/) lists the Philippines as a market, with methods such as GCash, QR Ph and over-the-counter payments. For example, it shows QR Ph at 1.50% (minimum PHP 15) plus a PHP 11 processing fee. A local gateway does not handle foreign sales tax for you the way a merchant of record does.

## The US LLC route

Some Filipino founders form a US LLC to sell through Stripe in the US. Two things to know first:

- **Mercury is out while you live in the Philippines.** Mercury lists the Philippines on its [prohibited countries page](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28771710754580-Prohibited-countries), based on residence, not citizenship.
- **Airwallex onboards US companies.** The United States is on Airwallex's [eligible countries list](https://help.airwallex.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4408591334937-Eligible-Countries-and-Territories). Approval is up to Airwallex.

Formation services we track include [doola](/provider/doola/), [Firstbase](/provider/firstbase/) and [Northwest Registered Agent](/provider/northwest-registered-agent/). We have not verified on their sites whether each one accepts founders living in the Philippines. A US company brings US filing duties and may have Philippine tax consequences, so talk to a qualified accountant before you form one.

## Banking and payouts

- **Airwallex:** businesses registered in the Philippines are on Airwallex's [eligible list](https://help.airwallex.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4408591334937-Eligible-Countries-and-Territories) and are onboarded to its Singapore entity.
- **Wise:** the Philippines is not on Wise's [list of places it does not serve](https://wise.com/help/articles/2978049/where-can-i-use-wise). Wise confirms business eligibility when you sign up.
- **Payoneer:** unknown. We have not verified Payoneer's eligibility rules for the Philippines on its site.

## Which option fits

- Selling software or SaaS to buyers abroad: start with a merchant of record. Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar and Dodo all list the Philippines or do not exclude it.
- Selling mostly to Filipino customers: a local gateway such as Xendit for GCash and QR payments.
- Planning to raise money from US investors: the US company route, without Mercury while you live in the Philippines.
