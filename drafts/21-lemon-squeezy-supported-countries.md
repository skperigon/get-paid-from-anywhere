Lemon Squeezy is a merchant of record for digital products: it sells to your customers, handles sales tax and VAT, and pays you out. Whether you can join depends on one thing: whether Lemon Squeezy can pay you.

## The rule

Lemon Squeezy's [supported countries page](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries) says it works with merchants and affiliates who can receive either:

- a **bank payout** in one of the countries on its list, or
- a **PayPal payout**, which it says is supported in 200+ countries and regions.

If your country is on neither list, the page says you will not be able to use Lemon Squeezy as a merchant or an affiliate.

## The India exception

India is on the bank payout list with a footnote. From May 2024, Stripe accepts new accounts from India on an invite-only basis, so Indian merchants can set up a bank payout only if Stripe has pre-approved their account. Without one, Lemon Squeezy says to use PayPal for payouts.

## Countries your customers cannot buy from

Lemon Squeezy accepts purchases from every country except those on its unsupported list. When we checked, that list was: Central African Republic, Cuba, Democratic People's Republic of Korea, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eritrea, Guinea-Bissau, Iran, Iraq, Lebanon, Libya, Mali, North Korea, Russian Federation, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan, Syrian Arab Republic and Yemen.

## How and when you get paid

Lemon Squeezy's [pricing page](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing) says payouts go out automatically twice a month, by bank wire or PayPal. Its [fees page](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees) lists payout fees:

| Payout method | In the US | Outside the US |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bank, via Stripe | Free | 1% per payout |
| PayPal | 50 cents per payout | 3% per payout, capped at $30 |

## What a sale costs

The platform fee is 5% + 50 cents per transaction. The fees page adds 1.5% for transactions outside the US, 1.5% for PayPal transactions and 0.5% for subscription payments. Its own example: a $20 product sold to a buyer in France, with $4 of VAT, has a platform fee of $2.06 and nets the seller $17.94.

## Something to watch

Stripe acquired Lemon Squeezy in 2024. In a [January 28, 2026 update](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/blog/2026-update), co-founder JR Farr said the team is building Stripe Managed Payments, acknowledged slower support responses and less frequent product updates, and said the goal is an easy way for Lemon Squeezy users to migrate. The post did not give a date for any change to Lemon Squeezy itself.

## Before you sign up

Approval is up to Lemon Squeezy. Country lists and fees change, so verify your country, payout method and product with Lemon Squeezy before you build your checkout.
