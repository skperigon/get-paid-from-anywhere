Short answers first, then the detail. Every fact links to the provider's own page and was checked on 2026-10-22.

## Quick answers

- **Is Stripe in Kenya?** Listed, with an Extended network label.
- **Can I take M-PESA?** Yes, through Paystack, which needs its own account.
- **Which merchants of record pay out to Kenya?** Lemon Squeezy and Polar list Kenya, and Paddle does not exclude it. Dodo Payments does not list Kenya.
- **Is Mercury an option for a US LLC?** Kenya is not on Mercury's prohibited list.

## Is Stripe available in Kenya?

Stripe's [global availability page](https://stripe.com/global) lists Kenya as **Extended network**, the same label it gives South Africa, Nigeria, Ghana and Cote d'Ivoire. Stripe does not explain the label on that page, so ask Stripe what your account will include before you build on it.

## Paystack and M-PESA

Paystack's [Kenya site](https://paystack.com/ke) lists card, M-PESA mobile money and Apple Pay. On its [Stripe and Kenya page](https://paystack.com/stripe/kenya), Paystack says:

- it was acquired by Stripe in 2020;
- you need a separate Paystack account to use regional methods such as M-PESA, even if you have a Stripe account;
- businesses based in Africa can accept local and international payments and get paid out in USD;
- some businesses use Paystack for local payments and Stripe for international ones, which requires a US company, and it points to Stripe Atlas for that.

We could not confirm Paystack's Kenyan fees on its site, so they are unknown here.

## Merchants of record for Kenyan sellers

| [Paddle](/provider/paddle/) | Not excluded | Kenya is not on Paddle's [unsupported countries list](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle). Paddle says it works with software businesses. |
| [Lemon Squeezy](/provider/lemon-squeezy/) | Listed | Kenya is on Lemon Squeezy's [bank payout list](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries). |
| [Polar](/provider/polar/) | Listed | Kenya is on Polar's [payout country list](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/supported-countries). |
| [Dodo Payments](/provider/dodo-payments/) | Not listed | Kenya is not on Dodo's [accepted countries list](https://docs.dodopayments.com/miscellaneous/accepted-countries-and-territories). |

Headline fees: [Paddle](https://www.paddle.com/pricing) 5% + 50 cents per transaction; [Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing) 5% + 50 cents; [Polar](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/fees) 5% + 50 cents on its free plan, plus 1.5% for non-US cards. A merchant of record handles sales tax and VAT in your buyers' countries, which Paystack does not.

## The US LLC route

- **Mercury:** Kenya is not on Mercury's [prohibited countries page](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28771710754580-Prohibited-countries), which is based on residence. Approval is up to Mercury.
- **Airwallex:** Kenya is not on Airwallex's [eligible countries list](https://help.airwallex.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4408591334937-Eligible-Countries-and-Territories). A US company is eligible.

Formation services we track: [Stripe Atlas](/provider/stripe-atlas/), [doola](/provider/doola/), [Firstbase](/provider/firstbase/) and [Northwest Registered Agent](/provider/northwest-registered-agent/). We have not verified on their sites whether each accepts founders living in Kenya. Get advice from a qualified accountant on US and Kenyan tax before you form a US company.

## Banking and payouts

- **Wise:** Kenya is not on Wise's [list of places it does not serve](https://wise.com/help/articles/2978049/where-can-i-use-wise). Wise confirms business eligibility during sign-up.
- **Payoneer:** unknown. We have not verified Payoneer's rules for Kenya on its site.
