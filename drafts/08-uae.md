Stripe works in the UAE, so most UAE founders can take card payments directly. The real choice is whether to handle global sales tax yourself through Stripe or hand it to a merchant of record. Facts below come from each provider's own pages, checked on 2026-10-29.

## Is Stripe available in the UAE?

Yes. The United Arab Emirates is on Stripe's [global availability page](https://stripe.com/global), with no Preview or Extended network label. Stripe's [UAE pricing page](https://stripe.com/ae/pricing) lists 2.9% + AED 1.00 per successful domestic card transaction, plus 1% for international cards and 1% if currency conversion is needed. It also lists USD payouts to a local bank account at 1% of payout volume, with a $25 minimum fee.

Stripe is not the merchant of record by default. If you sell software or digital products to buyers in many countries, you are responsible for their sales tax and VAT.

## Merchants of record for UAE sellers

A merchant of record sells to your customer, handles sales tax and VAT in their country, and then pays you. It costs more per sale than Stripe, but takes the tax work off you.

| Provider | UAE status (2026-10-29) | What the provider says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Paddle](/provider/paddle/) | Not excluded | The UAE is not on Paddle's [unsupported countries list](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle). Paddle says it works with software businesses. |
| [Lemon Squeezy](/provider/lemon-squeezy/) | Listed | The UAE is on Lemon Squeezy's [bank payout list](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries). |
| [Polar](/provider/polar/) | Listed | The UAE is on Polar's [payout country list](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/supported-countries). |
| [Dodo Payments](/provider/dodo-payments/) | Listed | The UAE is on Dodo's [accepted countries list](https://docs.dodopayments.com/miscellaneous/accepted-countries-and-territories). Dodo checks the ID country of every director and beneficial owner, not only where the company is registered. |

Headline fees: [Paddle](https://www.paddle.com/pricing) 5% + 50 cents per checkout transaction; [Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing) 5% + 50 cents; [Polar](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/fees) 5% + 50 cents on its free Starter plan, plus 1.5% for non-US cards. See [Polar vs Lemon Squeezy](/compare/polar-vs-lemon-squeezy/) for the differences.

## The US company route

- **Mercury:** the UAE is not on Mercury's [prohibited countries page](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28771710754580-Prohibited-countries), which is based on residence. Mercury requires a US company, and approval is up to Mercury.
- **Airwallex:** a US company is eligible on Airwallex's [eligible countries list](https://help.airwallex.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4408591334937-Eligible-Countries-and-Territories).

Formation services we track: [doola](/provider/doola/), [Firstbase](/provider/firstbase/) and [Northwest Registered Agent](/provider/northwest-registered-agent/). We have not verified on their sites whether each accepts founders living in the UAE. Since Stripe already works in the UAE, a US company is not needed just to get Stripe. It brings US filing duties and may have UAE tax consequences, so get advice from a qualified accountant first.

## Banking and payouts

- **Wise:** the UAE is not on Wise's [list of places it does not serve](https://wise.com/help/articles/2978049/where-can-i-use-wise). Wise confirms business eligibility during sign-up, and approval is not guaranteed.
- **Airwallex:** the UAE is not on Airwallex's [eligible countries list](https://help.airwallex.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4408591334937-Eligible-Countries-and-Territories) for business registration. Airwallex says it cannot process applications from locations not on the list.
- **Payoneer:** unknown. We have not verified Payoneer's rules for the UAE on its site.

## Which option fits

- Selling mostly to UAE buyers, or happy to manage tax yourself: Stripe directly.
- Selling SaaS or digital products worldwide and want tax handled: Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar or Dodo Payments. Check each provider's product rules too, since approval depends on what you sell as well as where you are.
- Want a US entity for other reasons: Mercury does not rule out UAE residents on its list.
