South Africa is in a better spot than most countries on this site. Stripe lists it, Paystack serves it locally, and every merchant of record we track will pay out there. The main question is which one fits what you sell and who your buyers are. Everything below comes from each provider's own pages, checked on 2026-10-22.

## Is Stripe available in South Africa?

Yes, with a label. Stripe's [global availability page](https://stripe.com/global) lists South Africa as **Extended network**, the same label it gives Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana and Cote d'Ivoire. The page does not explain what the label means, so ask Stripe what your account will support before you rely on it.

## Paystack for local payments

Paystack's [South Africa site](https://paystack.com/za) lists these payment methods: card, Scan to Pay, Ozow EFT, Capitec Pay, SnapScan and Apple Pay. Paystack says it is a Stripe company and has a Cape Town office. If your buyers are mostly South African, Paystack covers the local methods they already use. We could not confirm Paystack's South African fees on its site, so we list them as unknown.

## Merchants of record for South African sellers

A merchant of record sells to your customer, handles sales tax and VAT around the world, and pays you. For SaaS and digital products sold abroad, this saves you from registering for tax in other countries.

| Provider | South Africa status (2026-10-22) | What the provider says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Paddle](/provider/paddle/) | Not excluded | South Africa is not on Paddle's [unsupported countries list](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle). Paddle says it works with software businesses. |
| [Lemon Squeezy](/provider/lemon-squeezy/) | Listed | South Africa is on Lemon Squeezy's [bank payout list](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries). |
| [Polar](/provider/polar/) | Listed | South Africa is on Polar's [payout country list](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/supported-countries). |
| [Dodo Payments](/provider/dodo-payments/) | Listed | South Africa is on Dodo's [accepted countries list](https://docs.dodopayments.com/miscellaneous/accepted-countries-and-territories). |

Headline fees, from each provider's pricing page: [Paddle](https://www.paddle.com/pricing) 5% + 50 cents per transaction; [Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing) 5% + 50 cents; [Polar](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/fees) 5% + 50 cents on its free plan, plus 1.5% for non-US cards; [Dodo](https://dodopayments.com/pricing) 4% + 40 cents for US cards, plus 1.5% for international cards.

## The US LLC route

- **Mercury:** South Africa is not on Mercury's [prohibited countries page](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28771710754580-Prohibited-countries). Mercury bases the list on residence and requires a US company. Approval is up to Mercury.
- **Airwallex:** South Africa is not on Airwallex's [eligible countries list](https://help.airwallex.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4408591334937-Eligible-Countries-and-Territories), so a South African company cannot onboard. A US company can, since the United States is listed.

Formation services we track: [doola](/provider/doola/), [Firstbase](/provider/firstbase/) and [Northwest Registered Agent](/provider/northwest-registered-agent/). We have not verified on their sites whether each accepts founders living in South Africa. A US company brings US filing duties and can affect your South African tax position, so speak to a qualified accountant first.

## Banking and payouts

- **Wise:** South Africa is not on Wise's [list of places it does not serve](https://wise.com/help/articles/2978049/where-can-i-use-wise). Wise confirms business eligibility during sign-up.
- **Payoneer:** unknown. We have not verified Payoneer's rules for South Africa on its site.

## Which option fits

- Selling to South African customers: Paystack, for EFT, Capitec Pay and QR methods.
- Selling SaaS or digital products worldwide without handling foreign tax: a merchant of record. All four we track list South Africa or do not exclude it.
- Wanting direct Stripe in the US plus a US bank: the US company route, where Mercury is not ruled out by residence.
