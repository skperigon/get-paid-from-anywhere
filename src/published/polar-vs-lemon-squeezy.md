Polar and Lemon Squeezy are both merchants of record for software and digital products. Both take on sales tax and VAT for you. The differences that matter most for founders outside the US and EU are the fee structure and how each one pays you out.

## Fees

| | Polar | Lemon Squeezy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free plan rate | 5% + 50 cents | 5% + 50 cents |
| Paid plans | Pro $20/month: 3.8% + 40 cents. Growth $100/month: 3.6% + 35 cents. Scale $400/month: 3.4% + 30 cents. | None listed on the pricing page |
| International cards | plus 1.5% for non-US cards | Lemon Squeezy notes some payments may carry additional fees |
| Disputes | $15 | Not shown on the pricing page |
| Payout costs | Stripe fees passed through: $2 per month with active payouts, 0.25% + 25 cents per payout, FX of 0.25% to 1% | Not shown on the pricing page |

Sources: [Polar fees](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/fees), [Lemon Squeezy pricing](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing).

## How you get paid

- **Polar** pays out through Stripe Connect Express and publishes its own [payout country list](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/supported-countries). Its FAQ says this works in places where Stripe itself is invite-only, such as India.
- **Lemon Squeezy** pays out twice a month to a bank account or PayPal. Its [supported countries page](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries) lists bank payout countries and says PayPal payouts work in 200+ countries.

## Which to pick

If you sell enough each month that a paid plan's lower rate covers its monthly fee, Polar's plans can lower your cost per sale. Work it out with your own numbers. If you live somewhere that neither one lists for bank payouts, Lemon Squeezy's PayPal payout option is the one to ask about. Either way, confirm your country with the provider before launch.

More detail: [Polar](/provider/polar/), [Lemon Squeezy](/provider/lemon-squeezy/).
