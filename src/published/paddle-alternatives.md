Paddle is a merchant of record: it sells to your customer, handles sales tax and VAT, and pays you out. The main alternatives that work the same way are [Lemon Squeezy](/provider/lemon-squeezy/), [Polar](/provider/polar/) and [Dodo Payments](/provider/dodo-payments/). This page compares them on fees, payouts, seller countries and product rules. Facts come from each provider's own pages, checked on 2026-11-03.

## Why people look for an alternative

- Software only. Paddle says it works with software companies, including B2B SaaS, consumer software and games. If your main offering is human services or physical goods, Paddle says it is not a good fit. Source: [Paddle acceptable use](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/what-am-i-not-allowed-to-sell-on-paddle).
- Monthly payouts. Paddle pays out once a month. On the 1st, a balance over your payout threshold (minimum $100) converts to a payout, and Paddle sends it by the 15th. Source: [Paddle payouts](https://www.paddle.com/help/manage/get-paid/when-and-how-do-i-get-paid).
- Unsupported countries. Paddle cannot support sellers operating from a list of countries and regions, including Afghanistan, Belarus, Cuba, Iran, Iraq, Libya, Russia, Somalia, Sudan, Syria, Venezuela, Yemen and Zimbabwe. Source: [Paddle supported countries](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle).

## Fees compared

| Fee (checked 2026-11-03) | [Paddle](/provider/paddle/) | [Lemon Squeezy](/provider/lemon-squeezy/) | [Polar](/provider/polar/) (free Starter plan) | [Dodo Payments](/provider/dodo-payments/) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Base rate | 5% + 50 cents per checkout transaction | 5% + 50 cents | 5% + 50 cents | 4% + 40 cents for US domestic cards |
| International | No surcharge listed | +1.5% outside the US | +1.5% for non-US cards | +1.5% for international cards |
| Subscriptions | No surcharge listed | +0.5% | No subscription fee | +0.5% |
| PayPal | No surcharge listed | +1.5% | Not listed | +3% |
| Disputes | unknown. No fee shown | unknown. Not listed on the fees page | $15 | $30 |
| Monthly fee | None | None | None on Starter. Paid plans from $20 a month with lower rates | None |

Sources: [Paddle pricing](https://www.paddle.com/pricing), [Lemon Squeezy fees](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees), [Polar fees](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/fees) and [Dodo pricing](https://dodopayments.com/pricing).

Other fee notes from the same pages:

- Paddle says it has no monthly fees, migration fees or hidden extras, and offers custom pricing for high-volume or low-value transactions.
- Lemon Squeezy adds 5% on sales recovered through abandoned cart emails and 3% on affiliate referral sales, and offers custom pricing for products under $10.
- Polar's paid plans: Pro $20 a month at 3.8% + 40 cents, Growth $100 a month at 3.6% + 35 cents, Scale $400 a month at 3.4% + 30 cents.
- Dodo charges $1 per refund, 3% extra for buy now, pay later, and 1.5% for ACH and SEPA bank debits, capped at $15 or 15 euros.

### Worked examples

Our own arithmetic on the published rates, for a $30 sale, before tax and payout costs:

| $30 sale | Paddle | Lemon Squeezy | Polar Starter | Dodo Payments |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One-time, US card | $2.00 | $2.00 | $2.00 | $1.60 |
| One-time, non-US card | $2.00 | $2.45 | $2.45 | $2.05 |
| Monthly subscription, non-US card | $2.00 | $2.60 | $2.45 | $2.20 |

Only Dodo is cheaper than Paddle in these examples, and only on US card sales. With non-US buyers, Paddle's flat rate costs the least of the four.

## What you can sell

| Product rules (checked 2026-11-03) | Allowed | Not allowed (examples) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paddle | Software businesses, including B2B SaaS, consumer software and games | Physical products, human services not tied to software, marketplaces, advertising and marketing services |
| Lemon Squeezy | Software, SaaS, eBooks, PDFs, design assets, photos, audio and video | Services of any kind, physical goods, PLR and MRR products, among others |
| Polar | Software and SaaS, digital products such as templates, eBooks, PDFs, code, icons, fonts and design assets | Physical products, human services, marketplaces. Directories, eBooks, AI content generation, marketing tools and VPNs get closer review |

Each lists more categories than shown here.

## Which to pick

- You sell eBooks, PDFs or design assets: Lemon Squeezy lists them as acceptable. Polar also allows them, with closer review for eBooks.
- You want your money sooner: Polar lets you withdraw when you choose, and Lemon Squeezy pays twice a month.
- Most of your sales are US card payments: Dodo Payments has the lowest base rate. It does not accept new sellers from Pakistan, Kenya, Nigeria, Bangladesh, Egypt or Morocco.
- You sell software to buyers around the world: staying on Paddle may cost less, since its rate has no international or subscription surcharge listed.

Approval is up to each provider. Confirm your country and product with them before you build your checkout.
