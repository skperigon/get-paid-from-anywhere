Indonesian founders selling apps, SaaS or digital products abroad have more options than the "Stripe not available" posts suggest. Stripe lists Indonesia with a label, and all four merchants of record we track will pay out there. Facts below come from each provider's own pages, checked on 2026-10-22.

## Is Stripe available in Indonesia?

Partly. Stripe's [global availability page](https://stripe.com/global) lists Indonesia with a **Preview** label. India has the same label, and Stripe says new Indian accounts are invite-only. Stripe's page does not say what Preview means for Indonesia, so ask Stripe before you plan around it.

## Merchants of record that pay out to Indonesia

A merchant of record sells to your customer, collects and remits sales tax and VAT, and then pays you.

| Provider | Indonesia status (2026-10-22) | What the provider says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Paddle](/provider/paddle/) | Not excluded | Indonesia is not on Paddle's [unsupported countries list](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle). Paddle says it works with software businesses. |
| [Lemon Squeezy](/provider/lemon-squeezy/) | Listed | Indonesia is on Lemon Squeezy's [bank payout list](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries). |
| [Polar](/provider/polar/) | Listed | Indonesia is on Polar's [payout country list](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/supported-countries). |
| [Dodo Payments](/provider/dodo-payments/) | Listed | Indonesia is on Dodo's [accepted countries list](https://docs.dodopayments.com/miscellaneous/accepted-countries-and-territories). |

Headline fees, from each provider's pricing page: [Paddle](https://www.paddle.com/pricing) 5% + 50 cents per transaction; [Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing) 5% + 50 cents; [Polar](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/fees) 5% + 50 cents on its free plan, plus 1.5% for non-US cards; [Dodo](https://dodopayments.com/pricing) 4% + 40 cents for US cards, plus 1.5% for international cards.

## Local payment gateways

If many of your buyers are in Indonesia, you may want local methods such as QRIS, bank virtual accounts and e-wallets as well as cards. Xendit's [pricing page](https://www.xendit.co/en/pricing/) lists Indonesia as a market, with QRIS, virtual accounts from several banks, and e-wallets such as DANA. It shows QRIS at 0.70% (inclusive of VAT, for transactions above IDR 100,000) plus an IDR 4,000 processing fee. A local gateway does not handle tax in your buyers' countries the way a merchant of record does.

## The US LLC route

- **Mercury is out while you live in Indonesia.** Indonesia is on Mercury's [prohibited countries page](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28771710754580-Prohibited-countries), based on residence, not citizenship.
- **Airwallex accepts US companies.** The United States is on Airwallex's [eligible countries list](https://help.airwallex.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4408591334937-Eligible-Countries-and-Territories).

Formation services we track: [doola](/provider/doola/), [Firstbase](/provider/firstbase/) and [Northwest Registered Agent](/provider/northwest-registered-agent/). We have not verified on their sites whether each accepts founders living in Indonesia. A US company brings US filing duties and may have Indonesian tax consequences, so get advice from a qualified accountant first.

## Banking and payouts

- **Airwallex:** businesses registered in Indonesia are on Airwallex's [eligible list](https://help.airwallex.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4408591334937-Eligible-Countries-and-Territories) and are onboarded to its Hong Kong entity.
- **Wise:** Indonesia is not on Wise's [list of places it does not serve](https://wise.com/help/articles/2978049/where-can-i-use-wise). Wise confirms business eligibility during sign-up.
- **Payoneer:** unknown. We have not verified Payoneer's rules for Indonesia on its site.

## Which option fits

- Selling SaaS or digital products to buyers abroad: a merchant of record. All four we track list Indonesia or do not exclude it.
- Selling mostly to Indonesian customers: a local gateway with QRIS and virtual accounts.
- Wanting a multi-currency business account for an Indonesian company: Airwallex lists Indonesia.
