Stripe Atlas forms a Delaware company for a $500 one-time fee. Founders look at alternatives for a lower price, a different state such as Wyoming, or extras like a US address and tax filing. This page compares what each provider's own pages say, checked on 2026-11-09. Prices change and offers end, so verify with the provider before you pay.

This page is not legal or tax advice. State fees and state taxes are extra with most providers and are not listed here. Links to providers on this site are not affiliate links today. See the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) for how that would be marked.

## Stripe Atlas

Atlas forms a Delaware LLC or a Delaware C corporation. Stripe says the $500 fee covers incorporation with next-day expedited processing, state filing fees, the company tax ID (EIN), founder equity issuance, the 83(b) election filing, document templates and the first year of registered agent service. After the first year the registered agent costs $100 a year and renews automatically. Stripe also lists $2,500 in Stripe credits and partner discounts.

Stripe says you can apply from almost anywhere. Its [Atlas terms](https://stripe.com/legal/atlas) list Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria and the Crimean region of Ukraine as prohibited jurisdictions. Atlas fits founders who want a Delaware C corporation to raise money. If you want a Wyoming LLC or a lower first-year cost, the providers below are the usual options.

## doola

doola's Starter plan is $297 a year plus state fees. Its help center lists formation, EIN, operating agreement or bylaws, registered agent service and a virtual business address. doola says you do not need to be a US citizen and that it serves 175+ countries. Higher plans add tax filing: Tax and Compliance is $1,999 a year and Business-in-a-Box is $2,999 a year. Because Starter is billed yearly, compare its second-year cost with a registered agent renewal elsewhere.

## Firstbase

Firstbase Start shows an offer price of $99 plus the state fee, with a list price of $399. It forms companies in Delaware or Wyoming and includes EIN setup. Firstbase says a registered agent is required for incorporation and sells Firstbase Agent at $299 per state per year. It also says a US address is required. If you have none, its Mailroom service costs $35 a month or $315 a year. The pricing page says Firstbase serves 190+ countries. When the $99 offer ends is unknown.

## Northwest Registered Agent

Northwest forms an LLC for $39 plus state fees, with the first year of registered agent service included. After that the registered agent costs $125 a year, or $100 per state for five or more states. Northwest says you do not need to be a US citizen or set foot in the US to form an LLC. The EIN is not part of the $39 formation: Northwest charges $50 if you have an SSN and $200 if you do not. You can also apply to the IRS yourself, and Northwest notes that applicants without an SSN or ITIN cannot use the online form and must apply by phone, fax or mail.

## Clemta

Clemta's Essentials plan is $349 a year plus the state fee. Its pricing page lists company formation with EIN, bank account application support, a registered agent and a business address with mail forwarding (3 scans a year). The Pro plan at $1,068 a year adds annual state reports and federal tax filings. Clemta says it serves 170+ countries. Which states it forms in is not stated on the pricing page.

## Before you choose

- Pick the entity and state first. A Delaware C corporation is the common choice for venture funding. Many solo founders pick an LLC.
- Add up the first two years, not just the formation fee. Include the registered agent renewal, the EIN service if you need one, a US address if your bank asks for one, and state fees and taxes.
- Check the provider's restricted country list against your citizenship and residence before you pay.
- Forming a company does not open a bank account. See [US bank account for a non-resident LLC](/guides/us-bank-account-for-non-resident-llc/) for what banks require.
- Ask a tax professional about US filing duties for a foreign-owned company.
