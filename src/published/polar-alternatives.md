Polar is a merchant of record: it charges your customer, handles sales tax and VAT, and pays you out through Stripe Connect Express. The main alternatives that work the same way are [Paddle](/provider/paddle/), [Dodo Payments](/provider/dodo-payments/), Creem and Gumroad. This page compares them on fees, payouts, seller countries and product rules. Every fact comes from the provider's own pages, checked on 2026-11-23. Policies change and approval is never guaranteed, so verify with the provider before you rely on any option.

This page is not tax, legal or banking advice. Links to providers on this site are not affiliate links today. See the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) for how that would be marked.

Lemon Squeezy is not on this list. It says it is no longer accepting new sign-ups and will shut down on March 31, 2027 ([source](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/blog/saying-goodbye-to-lemon-squeezy)).

## Why people look for a Polar alternative

- **Your country is not on Polar's payout list.** Polar pays out only in the countries on its [supported countries page](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/supported-countries). Of the 16 founder countries in our table below, Brazil, Morocco and Nepal were not on it on 2026-11-23.
- **You sell as an individual.** Polar says individuals can sell if Stripe Connect Express supports the individual business type in their country. Polar points you to Stripe to check that. We did not check it per country, so it is unknown here.
- **Fees on international sales.** Polar's free Starter plan charges 5% + 50 cents, plus 1.5% for non-US cards ([fees](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/fees)).
- **Payout costs.** Polar passes on Stripe's payout fees: $2 per month with active payouts, 0.25% + 25 cents per payout, and currency conversion from 0.25% (EU) to 1% elsewhere.

## Payouts compared

| Provider | How and when you get paid (2026-11-23) |
| --- | --- |
| Polar | You start withdrawals yourself. Polar says it will pay out for you if a balance sits unwithdrawn for several months. Stripe payout fees are passed on. |
| Paddle | Once a month. On the 1st, a balance over your payout threshold (minimum $100) becomes a payout, sent between the 2nd and the 15th. You cannot withdraw on demand ([source](https://www.paddle.com/help/manage/get-paid/when-and-how-do-i-get-paid)). |
| Dodo Payments | Its pricing page lists standard payouts as free, and its FAQ says a $5 fee applies to payouts under $1,000. USD SWIFT payouts for non-US businesses cost $25. A payout schedule was not stated on that page. |
| Creem | On the 1st and 15th of each month, to a bank account or a USDC wallet. Bank transfer payout fee: 7 EUR/USD or 1% of the payout, whichever is higher ([source](https://docs.creem.io/merchant-of-record/supported-countries)). |
| Gumroad | To a local bank account in your local currency only, minimum $100. Where bank payouts are not supported, it pays through PayPal in USD with a 2% fee. On weekly, monthly and quarterly schedules each sale is held at least 7 days ([source](https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid)). |

## What you can sell

- **Polar:** Its [acceptable use policy](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/acceptable-use) allows software and SaaS, digital products such as templates, eBooks, PDFs, code, fonts and design assets, and premium content such as courses, Discord or GitHub access. It prohibits adult AI-generated content and AI relationship services, among others.
- **Paddle:** Says it works with software businesses.
- **Dodo Payments:** Its [merchant acceptance page](https://docs.dodopayments.com/miscellaneous/merchant-acceptance) says it only supports digital delivery, so no physical goods and no services delivered in person.
- **Creem:** Physical goods are on its prohibited list. Its [account review page](https://docs.creem.io/merchant-of-record/account-reviews/account-reviews) says every store needs a website on its own custom domain before it can accept live payments. Free hosting subdomains such as vercel.app or github.io are not accepted.
- **Gumroad:** Its [list of things not allowed](https://gumroad.com/help/article/155-things-you-cant-sell-on-gumroad) includes AI services (access to AI tools, chatbots, image or content generation), web hosting, event tickets and physical products.

## Which to pick

- **Polar does not pay out in your country:** Paddle and Creem cover the most countries in our table. Check Creem's partner note if you are in Pakistan, Bangladesh or Nepal.
- **You want the lowest rate on international sales:** Creem's 3.9% + 40 cents has no international card fee listed. Paddle's flat 5% + 50 cents also has no international surcharge listed.
- **Most sales are US card payments:** Dodo Payments (4% + 40 cents) and Creem (3.9% + 40 cents) cost less than Polar Starter or Paddle.
- **You sell eBooks, templates or design assets:** Polar, Dodo Payments, Creem and Gumroad all list digital products. Paddle says it works with software businesses, so check with Paddle first.
- **You sell an AI tool:** Gumroad does not allow AI services. Check the AI rules of whichever provider you choose before you build.
- **You do not have your own domain yet:** Creem will not approve a store without one.

Approval is up to each provider. Confirm your country and product with them before you build your checkout.
