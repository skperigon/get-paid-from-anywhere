Many founders outside the US choose between a Wyoming LLC and a Delaware LLC. This page lists what each state's own fee schedule says, checked on 2026-11-10, and which formation services offer each state. Fees change, so verify with the state and the provider before you file.

This page is not tax or legal advice. Where you live, where you do business and how the LLC is taxed matter more than the state fees below. Talk to a tax professional in your country.

## State costs compared

| Item (checked 2026-11-10) | Wyoming | Delaware |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form a domestic LLC (state filing fee) | $100 (Articles of Organization) | $110 (Formation, domestic) |
| Yearly state charge | Annual report license tax: $60, or two-tenths of one mill ($0.0002) per dollar of assets located and employed in Wyoming, whichever is greater | $400 annual tax |
| Annual report | Required | Not required for LLCs |
| Due date | First day of the anniversary month of formation | On or before June 1, for the prior year |
| Late payment | Reinstatement for tax: $100 | $200 penalty plus 1.5% interest per month on tax and penalty |
| Expedited filing | Same business day $1,400. Next business day $700 | LLC formation: same day $100, 24-hour $50 |
| Amendment | $60 | $220 (termination or amendment) |

Sources: [Wyoming fee schedule (effective July 1, 2026)](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/BusinessFees.pdf), [Wyoming business FAQ](https://sos.wyo.gov/faqs.aspx?root=BUS), [Delaware fee schedule](https://corpfiles.delaware.gov/Fee_Schedule/AugustFee2026.pdf), [Delaware LLC tax instructions](https://corp.delaware.gov/alt-entitytaxinstructions/).

## Wyoming details

From the Wyoming Secretary of State [fee schedule](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/BusinessFees.pdf) (revised June 2026, effective July 1, 2026):

- Articles of Organization: $100. Can be filed electronically.
- Annual report license tax: $60 or $0.0002 per dollar of assets located and employed in Wyoming, whichever is greater.
- Reinstatement for tax: $100. Reinstatement for no registered agent: $350.
- Series under a series LLC: $10 per series.
- Certificates of good standing can be obtained online at no cost through wyobiz.wyo.gov.

From the [Wyoming business FAQ](https://sos.wyo.gov/faqs.aspx?root=BUS):

- Every business entity must file an annual report to stay in good standing.
- The due date is based on the anniversary month of formation. Wyoming's example: a business registered on January 15 has its annual report due January 1 each year.
- An annual report may be filed up to 120 days before its due date.
- Annual reports can be filed online with Visa or Mastercard. E-filing is not permitted if the fee is greater than $500.

## Delaware details

From the Delaware Division of Corporations [fee schedule](https://corpfiles.delaware.gov/Fee_Schedule/AugustFee2026.pdf):

- Domestic LLC formation: $110. Delaware notes this amount equals the filing fee plus the municipality fee.
- Same day service for a formation: $100. 24-hour service: $50.
- Certified copy: $50 each.

From Delaware's [LLC tax instructions](https://corp.delaware.gov/alt-entitytaxinstructions/):

- All domestic and foreign LLCs formed or registered in Delaware pay an annual tax of $400.
- There is no requirement to file an annual report.
- The tax for the prior year is due on or before June 1.
- Missing it results in a $200 penalty plus 1.5% interest per month on tax and penalty.
- There is no proration. The tax applies if the entity is active at any time from January 1 to December 31 of the tax year.

Delaware also warns about deceptive mail and email asking for tax or report payments. It says to be suspicious of anything that does not come directly from the State or your Delaware registered agent.

## Year-one and year-two state fees

State fees only, from the sources above. A Wyoming LLC with little or no assets in Wyoming pays the $60 minimum.

| | Wyoming | Delaware |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formation | $100 | $110 |
| Each later year | $60 minimum | $400 |

Registered agent, address and formation service fees are not included. They often cost more than the state fees.

## How to choose

| Situation | State to look at first (still verify) |
| --- | --- |
| You want the lowest yearly state charge and have no plan to raise venture money | Wyoming, at $60 minimum a year vs $400 in Delaware |
| You want to use Stripe Atlas | Delaware, since Atlas forms only in Delaware |
| You want no annual report filing | Delaware LLCs pay the tax but file no report. Wyoming LLCs file one each year |

The state you form in does not change bank or payment provider country rules. Mercury, for example, blocks founders by country of residence regardless of where the LLC is formed. See [US bank account for a non-resident LLC](/guides/us-bank-account-for-non-resident-llc/).

## Before you file

- Re-check both state fee pages on the day you file.
- Budget for a registered agent every year, not only the state fee.
- Put the annual deadline in your calendar: the first day of your anniversary month in Wyoming, June 1 in Delaware.
- Ignore payment requests that do not come from the state or your registered agent.
