Lemon Squeezy is a merchant of record: it sells to your customer, handles sales tax and VAT, and pays you out. The main alternatives that work the same way are [Paddle](/provider/paddle/), [Polar](/provider/polar/) and [Dodo Payments](/provider/dodo-payments/). This page compares them on fees, payouts, seller countries and product rules. Facts come from each provider's own pages, checked on 2026-11-02.

## Why people look for an alternative

- Product rules. Lemon Squeezy prohibits services of any kind, physical goods, job boards, advertising, marketplaces, resell-rights (PLR and MRR) products, and NFT and crypto products, among others. Source: [Lemon Squeezy prohibited products](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/prohibited-products).
- Payout countries. You need a bank payout in a listed country or a PayPal payout. Indian merchants without a pre-approved Stripe account must use PayPal. Source: [Lemon Squeezy supported countries](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries).
- Fees that stack. On top of 5% + 50 cents, Lemon Squeezy adds 1.5% for transactions from outside the US, 1.5% for PayPal and 0.5% for subscriptions. Source: [Lemon Squeezy fees](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees).
- The Stripe transition. Stripe acquired Lemon Squeezy in 2024. In a January 28, 2026 post, Lemon Squeezy's co-founder said the team is building Stripe Managed Payments, acknowledged slower support responses and less frequent product updates, and said the goal is an easy way for Lemon Squeezy users to migrate. Source: [Lemon Squeezy 2026 update](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/blog/2026-update).

Sources: [Lemon Squeezy fees](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees), [Paddle pricing](https://www.paddle.com/pricing), [Polar fees](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/fees) and [Dodo pricing](https://dodopayments.com/pricing).

Other fee notes from the same pages:

- Lemon Squeezy adds 5% on recovered abandoned-cart sales and 3% on affiliate referral sales, and offers custom pricing for products under $10.
- Paddle offers custom pricing for products under $10.
- Polar's paid plans: Pro $20 a month at 3.8% + 40 cents, Growth $100 a month at 3.6% + 35 cents, Scale $400 a month at 3.4% + 30 cents. Organizations created before May 27, 2026 keep an Early Member rate of 4% + 40 cents, plus 0.5% on subscriptions.
- Dodo charges $1 per refund, 3% extra for buy now, pay later, and 1.5% for ACH and SEPA bank debits, capped at $15 or 15 euros.

### Worked examples

Our own arithmetic on the published rates, for a $30 sale, before tax and payout costs:

| $30 sale | Lemon Squeezy | Paddle | Polar Starter | Dodo Payments |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One-time, US card | $2.00 | $2.00 | $2.00 | $1.60 |
| One-time, non-US card | $2.45 | $2.00 | $2.45 | $2.05 |
| Monthly subscription, non-US card | $2.60 | $2.00 | $2.45 | $2.20 |

Paddle's flat rate is the cheapest here once international and subscription surcharges apply. Dodo is the cheapest on US card sales. If most of your buyers are outside the US, Lemon Squeezy costs the most of the four in these examples, tied with Polar Starter on one-time sales.

## What about Stripe Managed Payments?

Managed Payments is Stripe's own merchant of record product, and the product Lemon Squeezy's team says it is building. It handles sales tax, VAT and GST on sales to customers in more than 80 countries. It is open only to businesses based in the US, Canada, 31 European countries, Australia, Hong Kong, Japan and Singapore. It allows digital products only, excludes marketplaces, and Stripe reviews each business. We could not find its fee on Stripe's pricing page, so the fee is unknown here. Sources: [Managed Payments eligibility](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/managed-payments/eligibility) and [how Managed Payments works](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/managed-payments/how-it-works).

None of the 14 countries in the seller countries table above are on that list, so sellers there cannot use Managed Payments directly.

## Which to pick

- You sell software and want one flat rate: Paddle. It charges 5% + 50 cents with no surcharges listed and accepts sellers from most countries, but pays out only once a month.
- You want Lemon Squeezy's pricing with fewer surcharges: Polar. The free Starter plan matches the base rate and has no subscription fee. Not available for Brazil or Nepal.
- Most of your sales are US card payments: Dodo Payments has the lowest base rate. It does not accept new sellers from Pakistan, Kenya, Nigeria, Bangladesh, Egypt or Morocco.
- You sell eBooks, PDFs or design assets: Lemon Squeezy allows them outright. Polar and Dodo review them more closely, and Paddle is for software.

Approval is up to each provider. Confirm your country and product with them before you build your checkout.
