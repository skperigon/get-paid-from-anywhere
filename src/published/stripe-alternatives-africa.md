Stripe's own country list covers only a few African countries, and labels each of them in a way it does not explain. Founders selling software, SaaS and digital products often look elsewhere. This page covers the five African countries we track: [Nigeria](/country/nigeria/), [Kenya](/country/kenya/), [South Africa](/country/south-africa/), [Egypt](/country/egypt/) and [Morocco](/country/morocco/). Every fact comes from the provider's own pages, checked on 2026-11-24. Policies change and approval is never guaranteed, so verify with the provider before you rely on any option.

This page is not tax, legal or banking advice. Links to providers on this site are not affiliate links today. See the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) for how that would be marked.

Lemon Squeezy is not on this list. It says it is no longer accepting new sign-ups and will shut down on March 31, 2027 ([source](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/blog/saying-goodbye-to-lemon-squeezy)).

## Where Stripe stands

Stripe's [global availability page](https://stripe.com/global) lists five African countries, all with the label "Extended network": Cote d'Ivoire, Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria and South Africa. The page does not explain what that label means for sign-ups, so ask Stripe what applies to you. Egypt and Morocco are not listed.

[Stripe Managed Payments](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/managed-payments/eligibility), Stripe's own merchant of record product, lists supported business locations in North America, Europe and Asia Pacific only. No African country is on it.

## Merchants of record by country

A merchant of record sells to your customer, collects and remits sales tax and VAT, handles chargebacks and pays you out. You do not need a Stripe account of your own.

| Provider (checked 2026-11-24) | Nigeria | Kenya | South Africa | Egypt | Morocco |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Polar](/provider/polar/) | Listed | Listed | Listed | Listed | Not listed |
| [Paddle](/provider/paddle/) | Not excluded | Not excluded | Not excluded | Not excluded | Not excluded |
| [Dodo Payments](/provider/dodo-payments/) | Existing sellers only | Not listed | Listed | Existing sellers only | Existing sellers only |
| Creem | Listed | Listed | Listed | Listed | Listed |

- **Polar:** pays out through Stripe Connect Express to the countries on its [supported countries page](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/supported-countries). Polar says Stripe Payments does not need to be available in your country, because Polar charges the customer as merchant of record.
- **Paddle:** works with software businesses anywhere except the countries on its [unsupported list](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle). None of the five countries is on it. Mali, Libya, Somalia, Sudan, South Sudan, the Central African Republic, the Democratic Republic of Congo and Zimbabwe are.
- **Dodo Payments:** South Africa is on its [accepted countries list](https://docs.dodopayments.com/miscellaneous/accepted-countries-and-territories). Egypt, Morocco and Nigeria are grandfathered: merchants from before March 23, 2026 are kept, new ones are not eligible. Kenya is not listed. Eligibility follows the country that issued your government ID, not where your company is registered.
- **Creem:** all five countries are on its [supported country list](https://docs.creem.io/merchant-of-record/supported-countries), none with a bank transfer partner restriction. Creem says you cannot use it if your country is not listed.

## How you get paid

- **Polar:** passes on Stripe's payout fees: $2 per month with active payouts, 0.25% + 25 cents per payout, and currency conversion from 0.25% in the EU to 1% elsewhere. You start withdrawals yourself.
- **Paddle:** pays monthly once your balance passes your threshold (minimum $100), by wire transfer or Payoneer, and sends payouts by the 15th. Paddle says a $15 SWIFT fee may apply for certain countries. We did not find which countries on its pages, so whether it applies to these five is unknown.
- **Dodo Payments:** standard payouts are free, with a $5 fee on payouts under $1,000. USD SWIFT payouts to non-US businesses cost $25.
- **Creem:** pays out on the 1st and 15th of each month. Bank transfer payouts cost 7 EUR/USD or 1% of the payout, whichever is higher.

Your bank may charge to receive an international wire, so ask it before you choose.

## Paystack

[Paystack](/provider/paystack/) is a Stripe company that Stripe acquired in 2020. It says businesses based in Africa can accept local and international payments and get paid out in USD ([source](https://paystack.com/stripe/nigeria)). It is a payment processor, not a merchant of record, and its pages do not say it handles sales tax or VAT for you. A Stripe account does not give access to Paystack, so you need a separate Paystack account.

| Paystack pricing (checked 2026-11-24) | Local cards | International cards | Settlement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Nigeria](https://paystack.com/pricing) | 1.5% + NGN 100, capped at NGN 2,000 (NGN 100 waived under NGN 2,500) | 3.9% + NGN 100 | Next working day. Settled in naira by default, USD settlement in a public pilot with Zenith Bank domiciliary accounts |
| [Kenya](https://paystack.com/ke/pricing) | 2.9% | 3.8% | Two working days, to a bank account or M-PESA. USD settlement to a Kenya-based USD account |
| [South Africa](https://paystack.com/za/pricing) | 2.9% + ZAR 1, excluding VAT | 3.1% + ZAR 1, excluding VAT | Two working days. International payments settled in rand by default |

We did not find Paystack pricing pages for Egypt or Morocco, so Paystack's status there is unknown. Paystack's Nigeria page also says some businesses use Paystack for local payments and Stripe for international ones by registering a company in the US.

## The US company route

Some founders form a US company to open US banking and payment accounts.

- **Mercury:** Nigeria is on Mercury's [prohibited countries page](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28771710754580-Prohibited-countries), which is based on where you live. Kenya, South Africa, Egypt and Morocco are not. Mercury needs a US company and reviews each application.
- **Airwallex:** none of the five countries is on Airwallex's [eligible countries list](https://help.airwallex.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4408591334937-Eligible-Countries-and-Territories) for business registration. A US company can apply, since the United States is listed.

A US company brings US filing duties and may have tax consequences at home, so speak to a qualified accountant first. See [US bank account for a non-resident LLC](/guides/us-bank-account-for-non-resident-llc/) and [Stripe Atlas alternatives](/guides/stripe-atlas-alternatives/).

## Banking and payouts

- **Wise:** none of the five countries is on Wise's [list of places it does not serve](https://wise.com/help/articles/2978049/where-can-i-use-wise). Wise confirms business support during sign-up.
- **Payoneer:** unknown. We have not verified Payoneer's rules for these countries on its site.

## Which option fits

- Selling SaaS or software from any of the five countries: Paddle does not exclude any of them and Creem lists all five. Polar covers all but Morocco.
- Selling from South Africa: Dodo Payments is also open to new sellers.
- Selling mostly to buyers in Nigeria, Kenya or South Africa with local payment methods: Paystack, keeping in mind that tax handling stays with you.
- Leaving Polar: see [Polar alternatives](/guides/polar-alternatives/).
