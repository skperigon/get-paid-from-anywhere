Gumroad works in India: it pays out to Indian bank accounts in rupees. Indian sellers still look elsewhere for two reasons. The fees are higher than most alternatives, and Gumroad does not allow AI services. Below is what Gumroad and four alternatives say on their own pages, checked on 2026-11-21.

## What Gumroad offers Indian sellers

- **Payouts:** India is on Gumroad's [bank payout list](https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid), paid in INR to a local bank account. Gumroad does not pay out to Payoneer or Wise. The minimum payout is $100. Payouts to Indian bank accounts go out on Tuesdays, and on weekly, monthly or quarterly schedules each sale is held for at least 7 days first.
- **Checkout:** Gumroad says buyers in India can pay with local methods such as UPI.
- **Tax:** Gumroad says it has been the [merchant of record](https://gumroad.com/pricing) for all sales since January 1, 2025 and handles sales tax collection and remittance.
- **Fees:** 10% + 50 cents per direct sale. Gumroad's [fees page](https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees) says card processing (2.9% + 30 cents) and PayPal fees are not included. Sales through its Discover marketplace cost a flat 30%. Once your paid sales pass $20,000 in a calendar month, new direct sales that month drop to 5% + 50 cents.
- **Rules:** Gumroad's [list of things not allowed](https://gumroad.com/help/article/155-things-you-cant-sell-on-gumroad) includes AI services (access to AI tools, chatbots, image or content generation services), web hosting, physical products and event tickets.

As a rough guide, here is our arithmetic on the published base rates for one $20 sale to a US buyer paying by US card, before tax and payout fees: Gumroad $3.38 (10% + 50 cents, plus 2.9% + 30 cents), Polar Starter $1.50, Dodo $1.20, Creem $1.18 and Paddle $1.50. Your real cost depends on where buyers are, how they pay and how you get paid out.

## How you get paid

- **Gumroad:** bank payout in INR, $100 minimum, held at least 7 days on weekly, monthly or quarterly schedules.
- **Polar:** Polar passes on Stripe's [payout fees](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/fees): $2 per month with active payouts, 0.25% + 25 cents per payout, and currency conversion of up to 1% outside the EU. You start withdrawals yourself.
- **Dodo Payments:** regular payouts are free, with a $5 fee on payouts under $1,000. Dodo lists $25 for USD SWIFT payouts to non-US businesses.
- **Creem:** pays out on the 1st and 15th of each month, to a bank account or a USDC wallet. Its supported countries page lists a local bank payout fee of 7 EUR/USD or 1% of the payout, whichever is higher.
- **Paddle:** pays monthly once your balance passes your threshold (minimum $100), by wire transfer or Payoneer, and sends payouts by the 15th. A wire in a currency that differs from your bank's country goes by SWIFT for $15. Payoneer payouts carry Payoneer's own fees.

Your bank may also charge to receive an international transfer. We did not verify Indian tax, export or foreign exchange rules for any of these options. Speak to a qualified accountant before you choose.

## Product rules to check

- **AI tools:** Gumroad does not allow AI services. Polar accepts software and SaaS but lists AI content generation tools among products that need extra review before approval, per its [acceptable use policy](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/acceptable-use). See [selling AI apps](/sell/ai-apps/) for more.
- **eBooks and templates:** Gumroad allows them if you created them, but not resale of products you did not create. Polar lists eBooks as needing extra review.
- **Services:** Gumroad does not allow services fulfilled outside Gumroad. We have not checked each alternative's service rules for this page, so check before you apply.

## Which option fits

- Selling courses, eBooks or creator products to an audience that already finds you on Gumroad: Gumroad's INR payouts and UPI checkout may outweigh the higher fee.
- Selling SaaS or AI tools: compare Polar, Dodo and Creem, which all list India and allow software.
- Many buyers in India: Dodo publishes an India rate for UPI and Indian cards.
- Want one flat rate with no international card surcharge: Creem or Paddle.
