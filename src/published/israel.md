Israel is not on Stripe's list of supported countries, so an Israeli founder cannot open a Stripe account for an Israeli business. The four merchants of record we track all accept sellers from Israel, which covers most SaaS and digital product sales. Facts below come from each provider's own pages, checked on 2026-10-30.

## Is Stripe available in Israel?

No. Israel does not appear on Stripe's [global availability page](https://stripe.com/global), which lists the countries where a business can sign up for Stripe. The nearest listed countries are Cyprus, Greece and the UAE.

That rules out a direct Stripe account for an Israeli business. It does not rule out providers that use Stripe behind the scenes to pay you, such as Polar.

## Merchants of record for Israeli sellers

A merchant of record sells to your customer, handles sales tax and VAT in their country, and then pays you. It costs more per sale than a plain payment processor, but you do not need a Stripe account and the tax work is off your plate.

| Provider | Israel status (2026-10-30) | What the provider says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Paddle](/provider/paddle/) | Not excluded | Israel is not on Paddle's [unsupported countries list](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle). Paddle says it works with software businesses. |
| [Lemon Squeezy](/provider/lemon-squeezy/) | Listed | Israel is on Lemon Squeezy's [bank payout list](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries). |
| [Polar](/provider/polar/) | Listed | Israel is on Polar's [payout country list](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/supported-countries). Polar pays out through Stripe Connect Express, so Stripe Payments does not need to be available in Israel. |
| [Dodo Payments](/provider/dodo-payments/) | Listed | Israel is on Dodo's [accepted countries list](https://docs.dodopayments.com/miscellaneous/accepted-countries-and-territories). Dodo checks the ID country of every director and beneficial owner, not only where the company is registered. |

Headline fees on each provider's own pricing page: [Paddle](https://www.paddle.com/pricing) 5% + 50 cents per checkout transaction; [Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing) 5% + 50 cents; [Polar](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/fees) 5% + 50 cents on its free Starter plan, plus 1.5% for non-US cards; [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/pricing) 4% + 40 cents for US domestic cards, plus 1.5% for international cards and 0.5% for subscriptions. See [Polar vs Lemon Squeezy](/compare/polar-vs-lemon-squeezy/) for a closer look at two of them.

Approval also depends on what you sell. Each provider publishes product rules, and Paddle describes itself as working with software businesses.

## Banking and business accounts

- **Airwallex:** Israel is on Airwallex's [eligible countries list](https://help.airwallex.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4408591334937-Eligible-Countries-and-Territories), and Israeli-registered businesses are onboarded by Airwallex's Israel entity. Airwallex says being on the list does not guarantee approval and other eligibility criteria apply.
- **Wise:** Israel is not on Wise's [list of places it does not serve](https://wise.com/help/articles/2978049/where-can-i-use-wise). Wise [says](https://wise.com/help/articles/2977974/can-my-business-use-wise) it will tell you whether it supports businesses in your country when you add your details.
- **Payoneer:** unknown. We have not verified Payoneer's rules for Israel on its site.

## The US company route

- **Mercury:** Israel is not on Mercury's [prohibited countries page](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28771710754580-Prohibited-countries), which is based on where founders live. Mercury says it supports US companies founded by people across the globe, subject to its eligibility requirements.
- **Airwallex:** a US company is also eligible on Airwallex's list.

Formation services we track: [doola](/provider/doola/), [Firstbase](/provider/firstbase/) and [Northwest Registered Agent](/provider/northwest-registered-agent/). We have not verified on their sites whether each accepts founders living in Israel. A US company brings US filing duties and may have Israeli tax consequences, so get advice from a qualified accountant first.

## Which option fits

- Selling SaaS or digital products worldwide: start with a merchant of record. All four we track accept Israeli sellers, so compare fees and product rules.
- Want a business account for an Israeli company: Airwallex lists Israel; Wise does not exclude it.
- Need Stripe specifically: that requires a business in a country Stripe supports, such as a US company. Weigh the extra filing and tax work first.
